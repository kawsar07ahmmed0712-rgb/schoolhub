Highlights

A refreshed UI with more content and AI-driven helpers.

  • Attendance & Leave
    Teachers take attendance; students view history and requests.
  • Fees & Payments
    Monthly fees status for students; payments and receipts for admins.
  • Schedules
    Daily and weekly schedules for teachers; routine for students.
  • Notices
    Teachers and admins can publish notices for everyone.
  • AI Insights
    Dropout risk scoring and score forecasting, built from the bundled Kaggle datasets.
  • Gemini assistants
    Teacher notice drafts, daily-class suggestions, and student study tips.
  • AI Risk Sheet
    Teacher-only sheet with per-student risk scoring and auto sharing to Head buckets.
